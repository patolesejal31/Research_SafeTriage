# Clinician-Annotated Dataset Tier (Status: BLOCKED)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Status: BLOCKED**
> This directory is reserved for clinical ground-truth vignettes authored and validated by licensed medical practitioners under IRB / ethical research protocol.

## Why This Directory Is Currently Empty
1. **Clinical Informatics Integrity**: In accordance with the SafeTriage research methodology, artificial / developer-generated ground truth must never be passed off as clinician-annotated data.
2. **Pending Formal Panel**: Ground truth labels in this tier require independent double-blind scoring by at least two licensed physicians with inter-rater reliability measured via Cohen's kappa.
3. **No Fabrication Rule**: Never simulate or fabricate clinician data silently.

Until a formalized institutional review board (IRB) or clinician panel reviews cases, this directory remains unpopulated and explicitly marked **BLOCKED**.
